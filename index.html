Process

How it works.

A desktop, evidence-led route where the risk profile supports it — KPC can require valuation or inspection at its discretion.

01

Enquiry

Tell us the security, the amount and the exit. We give an early indication before any cost is incurred.

02

Title & security check

Desktop title, ownership and valuation evidence reviewed — site visit only where the risk profile requires it.

03

Offer

Terms confirmed in writing: advance, rate, fees and the conditions to completion.

04

Solicitor & legal pack

Facility letter and security documents issued through the appointed lender solicitor.

05

Completion

Funds released once security, title and legal conditions are satisfied.

06

Refinance / exit

We work the facility toward its agreed exit — refinance or sale — from day one.

Legal & Compliance

Business-purpose lending, clearly presented.

  • Kensington Private Credit provides unregulated, business-purpose secured lending to UK limited companies and body corporates. It is not consumer credit.
  • Kensington Private Credit is not authorised or regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority, and is not represented by the NACFB.
  • Borrowers are strongly advised to take independent legal advice before entering into any facility.
  • All facilities are subject to satisfactory title, security, valuation and legal due diligence. KPC may rely on desktop evidence where appropriate but can require a valuation or site visit at its sole discretion.
  • The borrower or charging party must confirm it is the legal and/or beneficial owner of the security and has full authority to grant the charge, with no undisclosed interests.
